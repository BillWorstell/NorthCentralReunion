Gerard "Jerry" Eamon de Valera Hughes passed away in Santa Rosa, California, on August 9, 2022, at the age of 78. He was an elected member of the Washington State Legislature representing Spokane before retiring to raise his children and later became a beloved public high school teacher. 
Life & Career Timeline:
Early Life: Born in New York on February 16, 1944, to Irish immigrant parents, Jerry grew up in Rockaway Beach and later relocated with his family to San Francisco, where he excelled at baseball at St. Ignatius High School.
Education & Inspiration: He attended Gonzaga University in Spokane, where a speech by President Kennedy inspired him to pursue public service.
Political Career: He entered the Washington State Legislature and served two terms representing Spokane.
Teaching & Legacy: After stepping away from state politics, he went on to teach history and become a beloved public high school educator (such as at North Central High School) before moving back to California in his later years.

Jerry Hughes served in the Washington State Legislature as a State Representative from 1979 to 1982 and later as a State Senator from 1983 to 1990. Prior to his political career, he taught history at North Central High School in Spokane, Washington. 
Biographical Details
Early Career & Teaching: Before entering state politics, Jerry Hughes worked as a public high school history teacher at North Central High School in Spokane, WA.
Political Run: Although he made an unsuccessful run for State Representative in 1976, he successfully ran for the State House representing Spokane in 1978.
Legislative Service: He served in the Washington House of Representatives before moving to the Washington State Senate, serving from 1983 through 1990.
Later Life: After stepping down from the legislature, Hughes went on to teach political science at Gonzaga University in Spokane.
